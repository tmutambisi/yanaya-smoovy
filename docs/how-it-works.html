How ordering works

Smoovy Baobab online ordering: what the customer does, what the system checks, and what your team sees.

1

The customer ordersOn their phone, in under a minute

Opens Yanaya

Logo animation, then "Start My New Order"

Order for Delivery

Where should we deliver?

Picks their suburb (delivery fee shown), types the address and drops a pin on the map for the rider.

Click & Collect

Who's collecting, and where?

Collector's name and collection point, with opening hours and Open / Closed shown.

Chooses how many bottles

Any number: quick buttons 10 / 20 / 50, or type their own. Bottles are $1 each.

Is it 10 bottles or more?The system checks this automatically
Under 10

Collect only

The order switches to Click & Collect and shows "Add 6 more bottles to get delivery" with a one-tap button.

10 or more

Delivery or Collect

Delivered to the door, or collected. Same price for everyone: $1 a bottle, no discounts for now.

Checkout: total shown in USD and ZiG, packed in crates of 10

e.g. 24 bottles = 2 crates + 4 loose bottles

💵

Cash on delivery

The customer pays the rider in cash when the order arrives.

🏪

Cash at collection

The customer pays in cash at the depot when they collect.

Nothing is paid online. EcoCash and card payments can be switched on later.

Bottles are reserved for each order

As soon as an order is placed, its bottles are set aside so nobody else can buy them.

Delivered / collected ✓
Bottles are taken from the oldest fresh batch first
Cancelled ✗
Bottles go straight back on sale
📦

Your stock, always up to date

The team logs each new batch with its expiry date, and records walk-in sales and spoilage, so the online stock always matches the fridge.

The app never sells more bottles than are in stock.
